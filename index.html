One rule, different questions.

A ratio stays a ratio.

For North, February margin is 20%. Year-to-date margin is 25%: add revenue and cost first, then calculate the ratio. The simple average of January and February margins would be 30%, a different answer.

Metrics compose within semantic constraints. Context and rule order matter. The model makes definitions explicit; people still agree the business meaning.

gross_profit = {net_revenue} − {sales_cost}gross_margin = DIVIDE({gross_profit}, {net_revenue})
