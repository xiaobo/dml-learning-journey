100 in gross profit on 400 in net revenue

January’s gross margin was 40% and February’s was 20%. February brought in three times as much net revenue as January. Divide total gross profit by total net revenue: 100 ÷ 400 = 25%.

Your team decides which sales and costs count. The model records that choice for the reports that use it.
